05 / VEHICLE

The car decision is mostly luggage and coordination.

Tight fit

7-seat MPV

  • Six passengers plus driver fills every seat.
  • Boot room is the constraint with the third row up.
  • Only sensible with disciplined cabin-size bags.
thin
Best single vehicle

9-seat traveller

  • More breathing room and a better luggage margin.
  • One rendezvous and one return plan.
  • Ask for a driver who routinely works the Kodai ghat.
Split-risk option

Two cars

  • Useful only when flights divide into two clean arrival groups.
  • More luggage room and schedule flexibility.
  • Twice the coordination and two return dependencies.

Round-trip quote model: one chauffeur vendor markets a single pickup-and-return booking and says its own package includes driver allowance, toll, fuel and GST. Treat that as a quote format to compare, not a market-wide rule. 8 reported moving
