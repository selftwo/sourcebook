# Fare payment interoperability

Northline Ferries · Operations · Last updated 12 February 2026

## What is accepted on board

Northline Ferries accepts the Meridian Card on every coastal route. The card is tapped at the
gangway reader before boarding, and the fare is deducted at the end of the journey. Paper
tickets remain available at every terminal counter and at the quayside machines, which take
cash.

There are 1.4 million active Meridian Cards in circulation as of 12 February 2026. An active
card is one used at least once in the preceding ninety days.

## Cross-border interoperability

The following partner schemes are live for cross-border tap-and-go on Northline services:

- Calder Transit, live since 4 August 2025
- Vantis Rail, live since 19 November 2025

No other partner scheme is live at this time. A card issued by a scheme that is not on this
list will be declined at the gangway reader.

## Agreements in progress

Northline Ferries and the Harbour Transit Authority signed a fare interoperability agreement
on 3 January 2026. Technical work is phased through 2026. Harbour Transit is not on the live
list above, and Harbour cards cannot currently be used to pay a fare on a Northline service.

We do not publish a target date for passenger availability until the acceptance testing for a
scheme has completed.

## Contact

Fares and ticketing enquiries: the terminal counter, or the operations desk at any quay.
