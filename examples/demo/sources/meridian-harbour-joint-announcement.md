# Joint announcement: Northline Ferries and Harbour Transit Authority

Issued 3 January 2026 by Northline Ferries and the Harbour Transit Authority.

Northline Ferries and the Harbour Transit Authority today signed an agreement covering fare
interoperability between the Harbour card and Northline coastal ferry services.

The agreement establishes the commercial and technical framework under which Harbour cards
will be accepted at Northline gangway readers. Work will be phased through 2026, beginning
with settlement messaging and followed by acceptance testing at a small number of terminals.

Neither party is announcing a date for passenger availability. Passengers should continue to
use existing payment methods until an availability date is published.

The agreement covers forty-two terminals across the two networks.
