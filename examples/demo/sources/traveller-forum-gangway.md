# Thread: Harbour card on the Northline ferry?

## Post 1 · 14 February 2026

Tried tapping my Harbour card at the Northline gangway on Tuesday. Declined twice. The
reader beeped and showed a red light both times. Ended up buying a paper ticket at the
counter, which took two minutes and cost the same.

## Post 2 · 14 February 2026

Same in December for me. Staff at the quay said the link is not switched on yet and told me
they get asked about it most days.

## Post 3 · 15 February 2026

Bring cash or get a Meridian Card. The quayside machines take cash and the Meridian tap works
fine on every route I have taken this year.

## Post 4 · 16 February 2026

Reporting the same from the northern route. Harbour card declined, Meridian card fine.
