# Harbour and Northline sign card interoperability deal

Harbour Trade Weekly · By R. Okonkwo · 6 January 2026

The Harbour Transit Authority and Northline Ferries have signed an agreement that enables
Harbour cards to be used for fare payment on Northline coastal services, the two operators
said this week.

The deal covers more than forty terminals and follows two years of settlement work between
the two schemes. Neither operator gave a date for passenger availability, and neither would
say whether acceptance testing had begun.

Analysts have watched the Meridian Card closely since its launch. An earlier industry
estimate put the number of active Meridian Cards at 1.9 million, well ahead of the operator's
own published figure.

A Northline spokesperson declined to comment beyond the joint announcement.
