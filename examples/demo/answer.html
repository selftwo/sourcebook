Can a Harbour card pay a fare on a Northline ferry right now?

No. The agreement is signed, the service is not live for Harbour cards, and the plan that works today is cash, a paper ticket, or a Meridian Card.

The short answer

Harbour Transit is not on Northline's live interoperability list, and a Harbour card cannot be used to pay a fare on a Northline service as of 12 February 2026.1 contested moving

For a trip in the next few months, plan to pay with cash, a paper ticket, or a Meridian Card rather than a Harbour card. thin

That second sentence is the author's inference. It carries no superscript because there is no source that says it, and the ledger records it as such.

What was actually signed

Northline Ferries and the Harbour Transit Authority signed a fare interoperability agreement on 3 January 2026.2 checked

The signed agreement covers forty-two terminals across the two networks.3 checked

Neither operator has announced a date for passenger availability.4 checked moving

An agreement is a framework for future acceptance. It is not acceptance. The distinction is the whole of this answer, and it is exactly the distinction the two sources below draw differently.

Where the sources disagree

One cluster was flagged on the topic northline.harbour.live and adjudicated both_stand. Both claims are true about different things, and a reader who sees only the second one turns up at the gangway and cannot board. So both are printed.

The operator, 12 February 2026

Harbour Transit is absent from the published live list, and a Harbour card is declined at the reader.1 contested moving

The trade press, 6 January 2026

Trade reporting describes the January 2026 agreement as enabling Harbour cards to be used for fare payment on Northline coastal services.5 contested

The reporting is not wrong about the agreement. It is describing what the agreement is for, in the present tense, five weeks before the operator published a live list that does not include Harbour.

At the gangway

Travellers report Harbour cards being declined at the Northline gangway in December 2025 and again in February 2026.6 reported

The same travellers report that Meridian card taps work on the routes they used.7 reported

Both are held at reported rather than checked, because a forum thread is real signal about what travellers hit and no authority at all about what the scheme supports. The shape is trustworthy. A count taken from it would not be.
